Capital in the domain marketplace is concentrating around names that signal **capability depth**, not just “AI” as a keyword. In 2026, buyers are prioritizing:

- Multimodal and multi-agent brand homes
- AGI / ASI narrative domains
- Infrastructure and platform names with category gravity
- Brandable one-word or compound .coms with investment upside

## Why “polyintelligence” fits the trend

As models specialize, the next competitive edge is **integration** — systems that coordinate multiple forms of intelligence. Domains that speak to that future outperform generic “ai + noun” constructions in brand storytelling and outbound credibility.

## Expired vs. premium inventory

Expired domains can deliver traffic, but premium category names deliver **positioning**. Institutional buyers increasingly separate SEO leftovers from brand-defining assets when building long-term portfolios.

## How to buy with confidence

Use escrow, verify WHOIS/history, and insist on a written transfer timeline. For high-ticket listings, personal agent contact remains the norm.
